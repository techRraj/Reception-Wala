FAQ

Doctor sahab ke sawal

Mera WhatsApp number mera hi rahega?

Haan. Number clinic ka hota hai, aapke naam par. ReceptionWala sirf uska AI layer hai. Kabhi bhi band karo — number aur patient contacts aapke hi rehte hain.

AI galat medical salah to nahi dega?

Nahi — ye hard rule hai. AI sirf reception ka kaam karta hai: booking, fees, timing, clinic info. Symptoms ka jawab hamesha "doctor check-up ke baad batayenge" hota hai. Emergency me hospital/108 refer karta hai aur aapko alert karta hai.

Patient data ka kya?

Data encrypted store hota hai, sirf aapke dashboard me dikhta hai, kisi ko becha nahi jaata. DPDP Act 2023 ke hisaab se consent aur deletion support milta hai. Health advice kabhi store nahi hoti — sirf appointment details.

Setup me kitna time lagega?

20 minute. Hum WhatsApp Business API ka number connect karte hain, aap timing/fees/services ka form bharte ho, done. Indore me hum khud aakar setup karte hain (pilot clinics ke liye).

Patient ko kuch naya seekhna padega?

Bilkul nahi. Patient wahi WhatsApp use karta hai jo wo roz use karta hai. Bas clinic ke number par message karo — jaise dost ko karte ho. "Kal 6 baje appointment chahiye" — itna hi.
